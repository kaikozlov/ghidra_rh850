Capture note
------------
A separate direct-Panda drive logger was attempted while pandad was already
running and stopped with Panda USB CHECKSUM_ERROR. It is not evidence. The drive
facts in this evidence unit come only from the normal loggerd rlogs reduced to
the tracked CAN-only artifact.

The operator was asked to exercise factory DRCC/LTA during the drive. The tracked
CAN itself proves movement, D state, MAIN toggles, and SET- presses, but does not
machine-prove an exact interval in which factory LTA lane centering was active.
Accordingly zero B6 is retained as a bounded active-LTA acquisition result until
a synchronized official FRC LTA oracle is captured.
