2026-09-22 exact-Camry normal-key proximity wake probe

Target
- 2026 Toyota Camry Hybrid
- Toyota-B relay-correct topology
- Vehicle started from the same deep-sleep state established by the immediately
  preceding quiet-baseline experiment.

Question
Does approaching and standing next to the vehicle with the normal Toyota key,
without touching the vehicle or pressing any control, wake a comma-visible
Toyota network?

Procedure
- Cooperative direct-Panda lease.
- Panda opened with configure=False.
- Board heartbeat watchdog disabled only during the direct lease.
- NOOUTPUT safety and closed intercept relay.
- Panda power-save disabled so all three CAN receive paths were live.
- Five-second zero-frame baseline required before arming.
- Operator was told to walk to the car carrying the normal key, stand next to
  the driver's door, and not touch the car or press anything.
- Probe automatically retained 30 s after the first received vehicle frame.
- No CAN data frame and no diagnostic request was transmitted by the host.

Result
- Armed UTC: 2026-09-22T20:45:16.657847Z.
- The network remained silent for 32.243788898 s after arming.
- First frame:
    bus2 0x45A/8 5a00a6020f0f0f0f
- Mirrored bus0 0x45A followed 0.062 ms later.
- Within 100 ms, the visible wake set already included mirrored:
    0x45A, 0x1BF, 0x620, 0x629.
- Native 0x00F began 303.507 ms after the first wake frame on bus2 and
  303.515 ms after the first wake frame on bus0.
- Within 1 s, 28 native IDs / 56 mirrored (bus,address) streams were active.
- The retained 30-s post-wake tail contains 5,320 frames total.
- Controller deltas during the capture:
    controller0 RX +2661, TX +0, forward +0, errors +0
    controller1 RX +0,    TX +0, forward +0, errors +0
    controller2 RX +2661, TX +0, forward +0, errors +0
- ignitionLine=false and ignitionCan=false at arm and at end.
- Safety remained NOOUTPUT.
- Panda ownership returned normally after capture.

Wake-set comparison to 2026-09-20 pre-brake state
- New normal-key proximity wake: 28 native unique IDs.
- Sep-20 pre-brake wake-active OFF state: 29 native unique IDs.
- Every ID seen in the new proximity wake existed in the Sep-20 pre-brake set.
- The only Sep-20 pre-brake ID not observed in this 30-s proximity tail was 0x129.
- Therefore the proximity-triggered state substantially reproduces the same
  wake-active OFF network that had already existed before the Sep-20 brake
  instruction.

Interpretation
OBSERVED: approaching and standing next to the car with the normal key, without
touching the car or pressing a control, is sufficient to wake a comma-visible
Toyota network from the verified zero-CAN deep-sleep state.

This is sufficient for the intended binary discriminator. It does not separately
distinguish RF key detection from any other no-touch effect of the operator's
physical approach; the treatment as executed was ordinary human approach while
carrying the normal Toyota key.
