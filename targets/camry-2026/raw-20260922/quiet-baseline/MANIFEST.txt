Question
Does the comma-visible Toyota network actually continue emitting CAN traffic in
true parked/deep-sleep state, or was the 2026-09-20 "pre-brake" traffic already
produced by an earlier vehicle wake event such as key/entry/door activity?

Interpretation
OBSERVED: the comma-visible vehicle network can reach a genuinely silent
parked/deep-sleep state. With Panda's receive hardware deliberately awake, no
accessible Toyota CAN frame appeared for 60.002 s and the underlying hardware
RX counters did not advance at all.

The prior 0x00F result should therefore be described as "SecOC synchronization
traffic while the power switch remained OFF but the vehicle was already in a
wake-active OFF state," not "0x00F runs continuously while the car is asleep."
The brake press itself did not begin that prior epoch.

The leading unresolved discriminator is which earlier event caused the wake:
key proximity/unlock, door opening/courtesy switch, key entering the cabin, or
another entry/start event. Toyota public/service material makes those event
classes plausible, but this capture does not identify the exact trigger.

Boundary
- This proves silence only on the comma-visible Toyota networks / Panda receive
  paths. It does not prove that every internal ECU rail or hidden internal bus is
  electrically off.
- Enabling Panda receive transceivers is itself observer intervention, although
  the successful run emitted no CAN data frame and observed no vehicle traffic
  or counter movement after enabling them.
- The operator's all-day untouched context is not machine-timestamped.
