2026-09-20 exact-Camry F33 startup PROGRAMMING race v2 — SUCCESS

Target
- 2026 Toyota Camry Hybrid
- EPS application F181: 8965F3307000 / 8A3113303100
- Known bootloader F181: 02 || 0x21 * 32
- Relay-correct Toyota-B topology, EPS diagnostic route bus0 0x7A1 -> 0x7A9

Question
Can a host already armed while the vehicle is fully OFF enter the F33 bootloader
on a normal brake+POWER startup by avoiding any application identity pre-read and
sending PROGRAMMING immediately after the first completed EXTENDED response?

Precondition
- Vehicle was confirmed electrically OFF immediately before arming:
  openpilot deviceState.started=false, Panda ignitionLine=false,
  ignitionCan=false.
- Vehicle remained in Park.

Strategy
1. Repeatedly transmit raw single-frame 10 03 at 20 ms cadence while the EPS is
   absent/asleep.
2. Wait for the first exact positive EXTENDED response on bus0 0x7A9:
     06 50 03 00 32 01 f4 00
3. Stop sending 10 03 immediately.
4. Transmit one raw single-frame 10 02 immediately after that completed 50 03.
5. Do not perform SecurityAccess, RequestDownload, payload upload, RoutineControl,
   ECUReset, memory writes, or persistent writes.
6. Independently identify the resulting diagnostic endpoint using read-only F181.

Authoritative timing from f33-startup-programming-race-v2.json
- ARMED: 306.582825 ms after probe start.
- First completed positive 50 03: 5128.182884 ms.
- Single 10 02 transmit: 5128.209499 ms.
- 50 03 completion -> 10 02 transmit: 0.026615 ms (26.615 microseconds).

Outcome
- The deployed v2 script crashed immediately after transmitting 10 02 because a
  diagnostic print statement referenced programming_after_50_03_ms as a Python
  variable instead of as the result-dict key. This occurred after the critical
  10 02 transmit and therefore prevented the script itself from recording the
  asynchronous handoff outcome.
- An independent read-only F181 probe immediately afterward returned exactly:
    02 21 21 21 21 21 21 21 21 21 21 21 21 21 21 21
       21 21 21 21 21 21 21 21 21 21 21 21 21 21 21 21 21
  i.e. the exact known F33 bootloader identity.
- Therefore the startup PROGRAMMING race SUCCEEDED.
- No SecurityAccess, authenticated RAM upload, flash access, reset, or persistent
  write occurred in this experiment.
- The vehicle was subsequently turned fully OFF; openpilot confirmed
  deviceState.started=false, ignitionLine=false, ignitionCan=false, with Panda
  returned to power-save.

Interpretation
Verified dynamically: an already-armed host can catch the exact F33 application
on a normal OFF -> brake+POWER startup and enter PROGRAMMING before the ordinary
startup/READY transition closes the handoff. No F181 pre-read, 0x030 trigger, or
arbitrary EXTENDED settle delay is required. The correct synchronization point is
the first completed positive 50 03 response.

Safety / recovery boundary
This race-isolation run deliberately stops at stock bootloader entry. A normal
full vehicle OFF cycle is the recovery path used here. Do not drive while the EPS
is left in bootloader state.
