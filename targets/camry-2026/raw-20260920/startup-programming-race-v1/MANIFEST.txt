2026-09-20 exact-Camry F33 startup PROGRAMMING race v1

Target
- 2026 Toyota Camry Hybrid
- EPS application F181 8965F3307000 / 8A3113303100
- relay-correct Toyota-B topology, EPS diagnostic route bus0 0x7A1 -> 0x7A9

Question
Can an already-armed host win the normal OFF -> POWER startup by repeatedly
placing the EPS into EXTENDED and PROGRAMMING before the READY-associated
application transition closes the programming handoff?

Procedure
- Vehicle initially fully OFF/Park.
- Host acquired the cooperative direct-Panda lease and disabled Panda power-save.
- No identity pre-read was performed.
- Host repeatedly transmitted raw single-frame 10 03 then, about 4 ms later,
  raw single-frame 10 02, with a nominal ~14 ms pair period.
- Operator then pressed brake + POWER normally.
- No SecurityAccess, payload upload, RoutineControl, reset, memory write, or
  persistent write was performed.
- After the race loop the host stopped session-control traffic and read only F181.

Result
- Final F181 was the exact stock application identity, so v1 did not enter bootloader.
- 167 native bus0 responses were exact positive EXTENDED replies:
    06 50 03 00 32 01 f4 00
- No 7F negative response and no 50 02 response was observed.
- First positive 50 03: 28552.883204 ms after probe start.
- First native bus0 0x51E: 28844.342946 ms, payload 20 00 01 00 00 00 00 00.
- First native EPS 0x030: 28927.859840 ms.
- First native bus0 0x51E with Ready bit set: 29428.885480 ms,
  payload 80 00 35 00 00 00 00 00.
- Therefore first positive EXTENDED preceded:
    first 0x51E by 291.460 ms,
    first EPS 0x030 by 374.977 ms,
    READY=1 by 876.002 ms.
- Positive 50 03 response latency from the latest preceding 10 03 was variable;
  median 12.021 ms, observed range 1.876..15.308 ms under the overlapping-request
  flood.
- For the first positive 50 03, the paired 10 02 had already been transmitted
  9.366 ms before the positive EXTENDED response completed. The next four sampled
  cycles likewise sent 10 02 4.875..8.266 ms before their 50 03 replies.

Interpretation
V1 is not evidence that the startup programming race is unwinnable. It is a
request-sequencing failure: the host deliberately overlapped 10 02 with an
outstanding 10 03 transaction, while the EPS DCM was still producing 50 03.
The fact that the EPS accepted and answered EXTENDED roughly 0.88 s before READY
is positive evidence for a usable startup diagnostic window.

Next experiment
Transmit 10 03 repeatedly only until the first exact 50 03 is observed. Stop all
10 03 traffic immediately, then transmit one 10 02 as soon as that positive
response completes. Do not pre-read F181 and do not add an arbitrary settle delay.
Then stop session-control TX and identify whether bootloader or application F181
reappears. No payload/SA/reset should be used in the race-isolation run.
