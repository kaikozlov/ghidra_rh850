## Evidence against equivalence

- **Corolla NRC `0x35` on SecurityAccess is non-diagnostic for the Sienna
  secret family** — it indicates the wrong secret was used, consistent with
  the Corolla being a different calibration with its own secrets. Do not
  interpret a Corolla `0x35` as "the Sienna algorithm is absent."
- The Sienna calibration has **no configured Dcm security gating**; that
  absence is calibration-specific and says nothing about whether the Corolla
  populates those tables.
