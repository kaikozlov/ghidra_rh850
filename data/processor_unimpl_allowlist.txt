# Accepted CALLOTHER user-ops used by instructions in the P1M-E firmware.
# Format: userop<TAB>reason
__disable_irq	DI privilege/interrupt-controller side effect is opaque; PSW.ID is modeled by the instruction
__enable_irq	EI privilege/interrupt-controller side effect is opaque; PSW.ID is modeled by the instruction
__nop	NOP intentionally has no architectural state change
__synchronize	pipeline/cache synchronization has no additional static-analysis state
